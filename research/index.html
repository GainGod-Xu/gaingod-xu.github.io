Research

Trustworthy world models for life science: current directions, organized from molecular recognition to system-level biology, each anchored by representative papers.

I build trustworthy AI systems to understand how biological interventions propagate across scales. The directions below move from molecules to interactions, networks, cells, and patients; each asks a version of the same three questions—understand, predict, intervene—and each is held to the same standard of interpretability, uncertainty, robustness, generalization, and mechanistic consistency. The home page shows how they build toward a world model, one scale at a time.

Interaction · Understand

Molecular recognition and biomolecular interactions

How do small molecules recognize proteins and RNA? ExplainBind predicts protein–ligand binding through explicit non-covalent interactions, so each prediction exposes its physicochemical determinants rather than a bare score. Ongoing work extends the same mechanistic framing to RNA–ligand recognition.

  • ExplainBind: Explainable Physicochemical Determinants of Protein-Ligand Binding via Non-Covalent Interactions. Meng, Z.†, Bai, Z.†, et al., Oldham, W. M.*, Meng, Z.*, Xu, H.*, Loscalzo, J. bioRxiv, 2026.
Interaction · Predict

RNA biology and modification

RNA modifications sit between sequence and function. EvoRMD integrates biological context with evolutionary RNA language models to predict modification sites interpretably, a step toward modeling how interventions on RNA propagate to cellular state.

  • EvoRMD: Integrating Biological Context and Evolutionary RNA Language Models for Interpretable Prediction of RNA Modifications. Wang, B., Zhang, H., Cui, T., Wang, X., Song, J.*, Xu, H.* Genome Biology, 2026.
Molecule · Understand / Predict

Multimodal molecular representation

Chemical structure is observed through complementary views: graphs, spectra, text, and 3D geometry. Aligning these modalities yields representations that transfer to property prediction and drug discovery while staying consistent with chemistry.

  • Advancing Drug Discovery with Enhanced Chemical Understanding via Asymmetric Contrastive Multimodal Learning. Wang, Y.†, Li, Y.†, Liu, L., Hong, P., Xu, H.* J. Chem. Inf. Model., 2025.
  • Multimodal Fusion with Relational Learning for Molecular Property Prediction. Zhou, Z.*, Li, Y., Hong, P., Xu, H.* Communications Chemistry, 2025.
  • Mat-Instructions: A Large-Scale Inorganic Material Instruction Dataset for Large Language Models. Liu, K., et al., Xu, H. IJCAI, 2025.
Molecule · Understand

Structure elucidation from spectra (NMRScape)

Determining a molecule’s structure from its spectra is a problem where a model must be able to check its own answer. This line builds solvent-aware 2D NMR prediction, an annotated experimental benchmark, and reasoning-based elucidation toward self-verifying structure determination.

  • TransPeakNet for Solvent-Aware 2D NMR Prediction via Multi-Task Pre-Training and Unsupervised Learning. Li, Y.†, Xu, H.†, et al., Hong, P.* Communications Chemistry, 2025.
  • 2DNMRGym: An Annotated Experimental Dataset for Atom-Level Molecular Representation Learning in 2D NMR via Surrogate Supervision. Li, Y.†, Xu, H.†, Hong, P. LoG, 2025.
  • How Well Can Off-the-Shelf LLMs Elucidate Molecular Structures from Mass Spectra Using Chain-of-Thought Reasoning? Wang, Y., Lu, W., Liu, L., Xu, H.*, Ling, H.* J. Cheminform., 2026.
Network · Understand / Intervene

Network medicine and systems pharmacology

Molecular interactions act through networks. Connecting binding and interactome structure to disease modules provides the route from a single intervention to a system-level response, and the basis for principled drug repurposing. Ongoing work builds affinity-weighted protein–protein interaction networks.

  • Systems Chemical Biology: Integrating Medicinal Chemistry and Systems Biology for Drug Discovery. Wang, R., Xu, H., Loscalzo, J. In Comprehensive Medicinal Chemistry, Elsevier, 2025.
  • Modern Computational Methods for Drug Repurposing. Xu, H., Loscalzo, J.* Drug Repurposing, 2026.
  • Deep Learning-Based MRI Model for Predicting P53-Mutated Hepatocellular Carcinoma. Jia, L., et al., Xu, H.*, Lei, J.* BMC Medical Imaging, 2025.
Methods · Trustworthiness

Trustworthy foundation-model methods

Scientific data are scarce and shift across experiments, so the foundation models behind these systems must adapt cheaply and remain reliable. This work studies which parameter directions matter during adaptation and how to keep multimodal models grounded in their inputs.

  • Not All Directions Matter: Toward Structured and Task-Aware Low-Rank Adaptation. Xiao, X., et al., Xu, H. ACL, 2026.
  • Staying VIGILant: Mitigating Visual Laziness via Counterfactual Visual Alignment in MLLMs. Xiao, X., et al., Xu, H. ECCV, 2026.
  • HyperAdaLoRA: Accelerating LoRA Rank Allocation During Training via Hypernetworks without Sacrificing Performance. Zhang, H., et al., Xu, H. Findings of ACL, 2026.
  • Sensitivity-LoRA: Low-Load Sensitivity-Based Fine-Tuning for Large Language Models. Zhang, H., et al., Xu, H. Findings of EMNLP, 2025.
Cell · Phenotype · Planned

Toward world models of biological response

The next step is to close the loop from molecule to patient: perturbation biology and single-cell response modeling, cross-scale state transitions, and inverting these models to design precision interventions. Each stage will carry explicit uncertainty and be tested for consistency with known mechanisms.
