01Vision

Why World Models for Life Science?

Research program →

Biological AI is increasingly powerful, but most models remain isolated within a single scale: molecules, proteins, cells, or patients. Living systems are connected across scales. A molecular intervention can alter binding, reshape interaction networks, change cellular states, and ultimately affect phenotype.

The challenge is not only to predict biology, but to understand how interventions propagate through it.

Understand

What biological mechanisms govern response to intervention?

Predict

What will happen under an unseen intervention, and how sure are we?

Intervene

What intervention will drive biology toward a desired state?

Across all three, one standard: interpretability, uncertainty, robustness, generalization, mechanistic consistency, and experimental validation.

His research develops trustworthy AI for molecular and systems biology, with a long-term goal of building world models that connect molecular interventions to system-level biological responses.
