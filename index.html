Micro-to-Macro Mechanistic AI

We build AI that links micro-scale mechanisms—model parameters, atomic interactions, molecular signals—to macro-scale behavior, across fundamental AI, life science, and materials science.

Led by Dr. Hao Xu, Investigator at Brigham and Women's Hospital and Instructor in Medicine at Harvard Medical School.
