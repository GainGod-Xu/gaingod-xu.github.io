XLab

News from XLab.

XLab’s core mission: Micro-to-Macro Mechanistic AI — building AI systems that connect micro-scale mechanisms to macro-scale behaviors and outcomes, from parameter dynamics in foundation models to physicochemical mechanisms in molecular and biological systems. In fundamental AI, the lab develops parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) and multimodal foundation models that connect micro-scale parameter dynamics with emergent model behaviors. In AI4Science, the lab combines physicochemical mechanisms with 3D geometric deep learning to reveal molecular-level principles governing biological function, enabling more interpretable molecular discovery and precision medicine.
